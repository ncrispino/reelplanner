# Design review — l2-upload-resume (round 1)

Judged against `docs/design-rationale.md` §2 and §4 and `skills/plan-to-video/references/style-guide.md` §11 and §15 (the grammar tightened after `videos/g1-bob-dylan-site/DESIGN-REVIEW.md`; this video was built before it), from `snapshots/contact-sheet-{1,2,3}.jpg` and six full frames (`frame-03`, `-09`, `-13`, `-15`, `-17`, `-18`), with source checks (`compositions/frames/*.html`, `.hyperframes/stage-snippet.html`, `.hyperframes/caption-skin.html`) where a midpoint snapshot cannot show a thing. Frame numbers are the storyboard's (Frame 1 = `01-hook`); snapshot files are zero-based (`frame-04-at-47.57s.png` is Frame 5). Sheet 3 carries Frame 19 twice (204.35 s and an end snapshot at 203.07 s).

## Verdict

The stage is honest in the one place G1 was not: every node sits at the same coordinates in all seventeen stage frames, every edge lands inside the node it points at, and the edge set grows only when a step draws it (Frame 5 shows one edge, Frame 11 four, Frame 15 six, and nothing appears before its word), so the map is learned once and trusted. It drifts toward generated design in exactly the classes G1 did: coral is never one thing (the caption underline, the ✱ spike, the recommended letter, three tags at once in Frame 19), a layer of 14–22 px mono sits inside and around the nodes (manifest rows typed into the navy tile, `409`, `7f3a`, `POST`, `BILLED`), and small drawings (a bracket, a loop arrow, a ring, six part boxes) stand where a word in a chip would. The single biggest improvement is to empty the nodes and give each beat one 26 px chip under its node, then make coral the stage's alone; the brownfield question (which edges exist today) is a second, smaller correction to the cast beat.

Counts: 5 must · 7 should · 4 nit.

- **The stage geometry is law and it holds.** Rail at x 80–580, slots 90 px on a 110 px pitch; Client SDK (700, 430) 270 × 110, Upload API (1030, 430), Manifest store (1360, 230) 280 × 150, Blob store (1360, 590) 280 × 110, Billing (1680, 230) 180 × 150, Sweeper (1680, 590) 180 × 110. Finding 6 changes only which edges the cast beat owns, not the inheritance.

- **The palette is clean.** Cream ground, tile nodes and slots, ink text, one dark tile (Manifest store, #181715) that the eye finds first, no gradient, glow, blur or heavy shadow anywhere; the hairline card shadow is the only elevation; empty rail slots as dashed hairlines.
- **Three voices.** EB Garamond captions and card labels, Inter slot and node labels at 30–32 px, JetBrains Mono for ids, bitmaps and tags. Keep the split; only the mono size needs raising (finding 3).
- **Captions are the script's sentences,** spoken words in ink, upcoming in grey, the current word marked. Keep the progression; only the underline colour (1) and the band anchoring (2) change.
- **Worked examples use real values** (`7f3a · 2 GB`, `111011`, `part four`, `2c91 · 26h`), and the bitmap-with-one-0 is the right shape for step 3 and step 6 (§11). Keep the values; move them into chips.

- **Why-nots as strikes and choice tags on the rail slot** (`range`, `client memory`, `lifecycle`, `×2`; `Postgres` on slot 1) are the right idiom; keep them with the case, size and colour fixes above.
