# Design review — g1-bob-dylan-site v2 (24 frames, three parts) — round 3

Judged against `docs/design-rationale.md` §2 and §4, `skills/plan-to-video/references/style-guide.md` §3–§6, §9–§11, §15 and §16, and `.hyperframes/dispatch-common.md`, from `snapshots/contact-sheet-{1,2,3}.jpg` and the full frames `frame-04`, `frame-12`, `frame-18` (storyboard Frames 5, 13, 19), with source and timeline checks on all 24 frames where a midpoint snapshot cannot show a thing. Frame numbers below are the storyboard's (Frame 1 = `01-hook`); snapshot files are zero-based (`frame-18-at-142.12s.png` is Frame 19). This is a new build, not a revision of the v1 reviewed above; v1's "Working — do not improve these away" list is treated as still binding and is re-checked at the end.

## Verdict

This looks designed for a reason, and more so than v1: the stage geometry is pixel-identical in all ten frames measured, edge inheritance is now honest in every frame (04–09 none, 10–11 one, 12–17 two, 18–21 three, 22–23 five and six, 24 all six at full ink, with undrawn paths held at `opacity: 0` rather than hidden by a dash trick), the coral hand-off is written into every step frame's timeline as an explicit node → edge → chip relay, and the three parts are 58 / 61 / 65 s of linear time — one sitting each, cut at a boundary where the stage state is continuous. It drifts in two places: the 47 s that v2 cut came disproportionately out of the beats that carry the *consequences* — nine frames are now under the 4 s floor, the six branch beats fell from 5.2–6.5 s to 2.66–4.02 s, and Frame 3's assembled map is on screen for 0.16 s before Frame 4 takes it away — and of the four "prototype" beats only three are prototypes: Frame 5 is 50 blank rectangles and 440 grey dots with not one word inside it, which is the counter drawing §15 bans wearing a prototype's frame. The single biggest improvement is to buy time back for the beats that pay off a choice: give the six branches 5 s each, give Frame 3's finished map a 1.5 s tail, and spend Frame 5's 11.4 s on a site map with real page names instead of a dot grid.

Counts: **5 must · 8 should · 4 nit**.

## Findings

### GRAMMAR (the fix belongs in `.hyperframes/dispatch-common.md`, `.hyperframes/stage-snippet.html`, or the style guide)

**1. must — Nine of the 24 beats are under the 4 s floor, and every branch beat is at half its budget.**
Frames 6 (2.656 s), 7 (2.677), 8 (2.725), 9 (2.680), 14 (2.844), 15 (3.676), 16 (3.563), 17 (2.482), 21 (3.852). Frames 6→9 are four consecutive beats inside 10.7 s — a cut every 2.7 s. In v1 the same six branch beats ran 5.48, 5.50, 6.53, 6.51, 5.19, 5.18 s. Violates §6 ("Deliberate cuts only: no beat shorter than 4 s, no 3 cuts in 3 seconds") and §9 ("Branch beats … 5–8 s each"); §16's closer is "3 s" and Frames 8 and 17 are under even that. The branch is where the reviewer sees what their choice does; at 2.7 s, with the tag landing at 0.23 s and the consequence chip at 2.06 s, it is a flash. Fix: in `STORYBOARD.md`, raise every branch beat to 5.0–5.5 s and every opener/closer to 3.2 s, adding the seconds as held read (the narration lines are already the right length at ~210 wpm); the parts then land at ~64 / 67 / 70 s, still inside §16's 60–75 s.

**2. must — Blur entrances and an idle scale drift are back in the first two frames.**
`01-hook.html` lines 197 and 207: `40` enters from `scale: 1.35, filter: "blur(14px)"` and `600` from `x: 96, filter: "blur(10px)"`; line 219 then moves the whole lockup `y: -72, scale: 0.92` over 2.2 s. `02-template.html` line 95: `tl.fromTo(drift, {scale:1}, {scale:1.015, duration: 8.182, ease:"none"})` — a continuous 1.5 % zoom across the entire prototype shot. Violates §2 ("No gradients, glows, blur, tilt, bokeh, music") and §4.2 and §4.11 ("a decorative animation"). v1's hook used a scale entrance but no blur (`git show <first>:…/01-hook.html` line 204), so the blur is new in v2. Fix: drop `filter` from both entrances (keep the opacity + y settle), delete the drift tween, and add "no blur filters, no idle camera drift or Ken Burns on a mock" to the dispatch's Motion doctrine line so the next worker cannot reintroduce them.

**3. should — The worked-example chip has three different forms across frames.**
`10-step-2` and `14/15-branch-2` draw the chip as a 40–44 px tile pill with a hairline border (`background:#EFE9DE; border:1px solid rgba(20,20,19,0.12); border-radius:6–8px`); `12-step-3`, `18-step-4`, `22-step-5` and `23-step-6` draw it as bare 26 px mono text with no box. Both appear on the contact sheets (sheet 2 tile 1 vs tile 3; sheet 3 tile 2 vs tile 4). The rejected-alternative ghost is equally split: `rgba(20,20,19,0.62)` in Frames 4 and 10, full ink `#141413` in Frames 12 and 22. §15 names the chip as a grammar element ("a single mono chip of ≤ 5 words at a fixed offset below the node") but never says what a chip looks like, so each worker invented one. Violates §4.12 (no reason for the difference) and §2 ("positions never change between frames"). Fix: add `.FID-chip` and `.FID-ghost` to `stage-snippet.html` with one definition each (pill or bare — pick the bare 26 px mono, it is quieter) and require frames to use them.

**4. should — Inherited edges sit at opacity 0.35, below the project's own contrast floor.**
`.FID-edges path.dim { opacity:0.35 }` in `stage-snippet.html` and in all 19 frames that carry edges. Ink `#141413` at 0.35 on cream resolves to about `rgb(168,168,165)`, ≈ 2.5:1 against `#FAF9F5` — under the 3:1 gate for a 3 px graphical object, and directly against the dispatch's own rule "Dimmed elements keep ≥ 0.72 opacity (contrast gate)". The dim edges are the plan's memory of what has been built; at 0.35 they nearly vanish on the contact sheets. Fix: `path.dim { opacity: 0.55 }` in the snippet and every frame (the active coral edge at full strength is still an obvious step up), or dim by stroke colour instead.

**5. should — §16 has no rule for how long both prototypes must be complete together.**
Frame 13: option B's page completes at 9.22 s + 0.5 s and the frame ends at 12.564 s, so the two era pages are both finished and comparable for 2.8 s, of which 1.9 s has the coral border. Frame 19 manages 4.9 s, Frame 5 about 4.9 s. A side-by-side comparison the reviewer is asked to judge cannot be read in under three seconds. §16 says "Two prototypes side by side at equal size" but sets no floor. Fix: write into §16 — "both prototypes are complete and co-visible for at least 4 s before the frame ends, and the recommended border lands with at least 2 s of held read after it"; then extend Frame 13 by ~1.5 s or move option B's reveal 1 s earlier.

**6. should — The stage snippet still tells workers they may put 20 px rows inside the navy node.**
`stage-snippet.html`, the comment under the node block: "The Content node is navy because it is the data surface: small mono rows (20px, cream) can sit inside it under the label". This contradicts §15 ("Nodes are a title, nothing else" and "Mono text is never below 26 px") and is the exact instruction v1's finding 7 asked to correct; it survived into v2 unchanged. No frame acts on it today, which is luck, not design. Fix: replace the sentence with "The Content node is navy because it is the data surface. Nothing goes inside a node but its title; the worked example is a chip 16 px below it."

**7. nit — The ✱ kicker mark is missing on one frame only.**
Frame 1 renders `✱ Part 1 of 3`, Frame 17 `✱ PART 3 OF 3`, Frames 3, 5, 11, 13, 19, 24 all carry the ✱; Frame 9 (`09-opener-2.html`) renders `PART 2 OF 3` with no mark (sheet 1 tile 9). Violates §4.12. Fix: add the ink ✱ span to Frame 9's kicker.

**8. nit — `STORYBOARD.md`'s scene lines for Frames 5 and 13 still describe the pre-§16 layout.**
Both read "two … boxes side by side **under the diagram** (700–1240 and 1320–1860, y 640–870)". The build is correctly rail-only, and the dispatch says so, but the storyboard is the record a future run reads. ## The series and the prototype format

**What works.** The three-part cut is the strongest thing in v2. The parts run 57.9 / 61.0 / 65.1 s linear and 55.2 / 57.3 / 61.2 s watched (`plan-map.json` chapters) — one sitting each, one decision each, one quick check in the whole video and none in the last 20 s. The boundaries are honestly continuous: Frame 8 and Frame 9 carry the identical stage (slot 1 filled and tagged, Content at full ink, zero edges), as do Frames 16 and 17 (slots 1–3 tagged, two dim edges); node and rail coordinates are byte-identical in all ten frames measured, so a viewer who stops after part two and comes back to part three is looking at the same picture they left. The rail-only decision frame is the right call and it is executed exactly: Frames 5, 13 and 19 drop every node and edge, keep the rail with the current step lit and the earlier steps' tags in ink, and the next frame (6, 14, 20) restores the diagram at the same coordinates with the correct inherited edge set. Frames 13 and 19 are real prototypes — Frame 19's book-like page has a serif title, a reading column, a nine-row track table and a small image box, and Frame 13's era page shows the year strip and three linked event rows against a page missing exactly those two things. A reviewer can answer "which of these do I want" from either of them without the narration.

**What does not.** (a) The openers repeat their closers verbatim (finding 12), so the seam costs 11 s of the 184 and re-explains where §16 says it must not. (b) The consequence beats got the cut: parts are at budget on words (195 / 198 / 197, against §16's ~210) but the seconds were taken out of the branches and closers rather than out of explanation, which is the opposite of §16's "Cut before shortening explanations" — the explanations are intact and the payoffs are starved (findings 1, 15). (c) Frame 5 is a prototype in name only (finding 9); the storyboard even labels it "PROTOTYPE of scope" while what it renders is a tile count. (d) The second mock is under construction for most of each decision frame, so the side-by-side the reviewer is asked to judge exists for 2.8–4.9 s (finding 5).

**Rules to write into §16 for the next build.**
1. *A prototype contains real words.* At least one real heading and one real label per mock, taken from the plan's own content; grey bars stand in for running text only. A mock with no readable text is a diagram of a count, and §15 already bans those.
2. *Both prototypes complete, both visible, for ≥ 4 s;* the recommended border lands with ≥ 2 s of held read after it.
3. *A part opener names what the previous part decided,* in the same sentence that names the steps ahead; it never repeats the closer's sentence. A closer's on-screen line is at most three words — the caption carries the sentence.
4. *Branch beats are 5–8 s even in a series* (this is §9 and it is not relaxed by the shorter parts); the last reveal in any beat lands at least 2 s before the cut.

## Working — do not regress these

- **Edge inheritance is now honest everywhere.** Frames 4–9 carry no visible edge, 10–11 one, 12–17 two, 18–21 three, 22 five, 23–24 six; undrawn paths are held at `opacity: 0` (`08`/`09` line 32, `20`/`21` `class="off"`) rather than hidden by the dash pattern, so v1's finding 16 (the stray tail on the cast frame) has not come back — `03-cast.html` sets every path to `opacity: 0` at build and to 0.35 only at its own draw time. Frame 24 lifts all six from 0.35 to full ink at 0.61 s and holds still from 4.21 s to 10 s.
- **The coral hand-off is written down in every step frame's timeline** as node border → edge stroke → chip, with the previous holder tweened back to ink in the same call (`10-step-2` 0.76 → 1.48 → 1.68 → 12.21; `22-step-5` 0.74 → 1.63 → 3.54 → 4.82). Every frame resolves to exactly one coral at any instant, with the ✱ mark, the option letters and the caption underline all ink. This is the hardest rule in the project and it is being kept.
- **Stage geometry and the palette.** Rail at 80/150, slots on a 110 px pitch; Content 700/430, Data build 1030/430, Generator 1360/230, Search 1360/590, Hosting 1680/230, Design 1680/590 — identical in Frames 3, 4, 6, 10, 12, 14, 18, 20, 22, 24. `.navy.dim { opacity: 1 }` is present in every frame that has the node. No gradient, glow, tilt or heavy shadow; the one hairline card shadow is unchanged.
- **The caption skin.** Band 880–1040 with the card's bottom pinned at 1040 and 46 px EB Garamond, spoken words in ink and the current word underlined in ink, upcoming in grey. Nothing in any frame sits below y 900 (the deepest element is Frame 18's chip at top 830).
- **Type and tags.** No `font-size` below 26 px anywhere outside a prototype's own page content; slot tags are sentence case and ≤ 12 characters with the same words in the branch and resolved frames (`Ten albums` in 6, 8, 9, 10–24; `Timeline` / `No timeline` in 14, 15, 16–24; `Book-like` / `Magazine` in 20, 21, 22–24), and the resolved frame shows them in ink while the branch frame's new tag is the one coral. One spelling per worked value holds: `10 · 40 · 30` in Frames 4 and 6, `40 · 400 · 30` in Frame 7.
- **Why-nots as strikes** (`CMS`, `templates`, `Next.js`, `UI kit`, `search service`) and the hook's `40 / 600` lockup are both still right; keep them (the hook needs only its blur removed, not a redesign).
