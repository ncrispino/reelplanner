## Intent

A short worked example of the diagram kit (`docs/diagram-kit.md`): a world of three actors, each with an item and a
locked slot, the ring of who can deliver to whom, a shared area, two ways to finish, and a check. Every scene is
`scenes/NN-slug.html`; `reelplanner diagram` draws the world from `world.json` and puts each move on its word.

## Customizations

- Ask: "a reusable diagram explainer kit plus one short example video built with it, so other users get nicely designed, smooth, non-static explainer videos without much thought"
- Medium: diagram
- Layouts: a title card; three areas side by side over a shared area; the same pieces as a ring with arrows between
- Main transition: cut (the stage is one place, and each scene begins where the one before ended, so a cut is invisible)
