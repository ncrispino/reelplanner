**Project record:** `packages/player/.reelplanning/`
**Kind:** brownfield (the page exists; this changes it)
**Stage:** `layout` — the artefact is the review page itself, drawn as a wireframe. See
`templates/reelplanning/theme/stages/layout.html` and `system.json → stage.why`.

## What this video is

The review page is where these videos get watched. This plan fixes it, so the video is
watched in the thing it is about: a reviewer opens the page, and the page they are looking
at is on screen being rearranged. Every step beat is the regions travelling to where the
step puts them, which means the motion and the sentence are the same event — there is no
beat here that is text over a still picture.

## Who it is for

The one person who has to approve a layout change: they already use the page, they know
what annoys them about it, and they have three calls to make.

## What it must land

1. The faults are **measured, not asserted** — four hundred pixels of nothing is drawn as
   four hundred pixels of nothing.
2. The three open questions are real forks with a cost on both sides.
3. It ends resolved: six steps, three calls, approve or send back.

## Out of scope

The palette, the type and the annotation tools. This plan is about where things sit.
