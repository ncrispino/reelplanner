# SCRIPT — reel-pane-explainer (one chapter, about a minute)

**Voice:** am_michael (Kokoro, local; sped ×1.25 after synthesis)
**Voice settings:** default
**Voice direction:** Plain, friendly and quick. Showing a colleague a feature on your screen.

---

## Line 1 — Watch it beside the conversation (Frame 1)

**Delivery:** Plain.

    A reelplanner video doesn't have to send you to a browser. With the plugin installed, /reel plays it in a pane right beside your conversation with Claude.

## Line 2 — Open a plan (Frame 2)

**Delivery:** Plain.

    Type /reel to list the plans that have a video, newest first, with their open choices. Pick one, or name it, and it starts playing at once.

## Line 3 — It stops at each choice (Frame 3)

**Delivery:** Plain.

    At each open choice the video stops. The question and its options sit under the picture, with the recommended one marked. Press a number to answer, m to leave a comment at that moment, and n or b to jump between stops.

## Line 4 — Send it back (Frame 4)

**Delivery:** Plain.

    The last stop sums up your review. Send it. Approve, and Claude builds the plan. Ask for changes, and Claude revises it, and the pane plays just what changed.

## Line 5 — Where the picture shows (Frame 5)

**Delivery:** Plain.

    In kitty or Ghostty the picture plays right in the terminal. Anywhere else, the pane opens the browser player and keeps the choices, comments and Send.
