What happens, in order, when the next three walkthroughs miss the bar, and what the review page would show instead.

The bar

Over the next three walkthroughs, both: a pause holds you at least five seconds (the middle value across its choices), and at least one flag, answer in your own words or written comment. Today: a third of a second, and nothing. Measured from the time each pause began (shownAt) to each answer (judgedAt); reel memory shows it on its after-build line.

If either half is missed

  1. reel status says: walkthroughs: still accepted without a look (3 of 3), a plan to drop the walkthrough video is due.
  2. The agent writes that plan and its plan video, and you review it like any other: its questions, your comments, approve or ask for changes.
  3. Only if you approve it does anything change. Ask for changes, or leave it unanswered, and walkthrough videos stay exactly as they are.
