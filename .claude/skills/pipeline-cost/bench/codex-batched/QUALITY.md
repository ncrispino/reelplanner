# Prespecified quality and timing rubric

An independent evaluator records pass/fail separately for every gate below, with artifact references
and findings. No total quality score may compensate for a failed gate. Inspect the immutable sources
and screenshots from each showing, not merely the final revised files or authored layout tags.

Every gate needs evidence-file paths and frame IDs supporting its specific facts. Where feasible,
blind evaluators to condition by presenting coded A/B artifacts rather than before/after labels.
For each of the six required cases and all ten option consequences, two independent evaluators record
three separate relative judgments: clarity of input→inference→result; readability with caption and
answer overlays; visual usefulness of the relationship. Each judgment is weaker/equivalent/stronger,
with material/cosmetic severity, paired artifact paths/frame IDs and a concise reason. A material
weaker after cell blocks the quality claim. Resolve disagreement with explicit evidence and
adjudication, never by averaging. Preserve raw judgments and unresolved findings. Record actual
visual-form counts and largest form share for both conditions; authored tag counts are separate.
The paired result is attached to both trials in the matched pair when filling their gate vectors.

Record raw seconds for request→first showing and successful review POST→revised showing. Total is
the sum of these two episodes, excluding synthetic reviewer delivery latency. All four ABBA trials
must complete both showings and pass every gate. A cap is a censored failure, never an excluded fast
run. Missing model exports mean unknown usage, not zero.

The pilot qualifies only if the after median total is at least 20% lower, after is faster in BOTH
matched pairs, and neither after episode median is more than 5% slower. Report individual seconds,
medians, relative differences, full gate vectors, command timings, usage and actual visual variety.
This establishes only a matched synthetic pilot; repeat qualifying pairs before claiming production
reliability. Keep model/effort, pace, permissions, source evidence, outline and checks fixed.
