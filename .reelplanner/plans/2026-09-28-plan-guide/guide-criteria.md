# What the guide and the review page must be: the owner's criteria

Gathered from the owner's own words (2026-09-28 to 2026-09-30) and the decisions they made. Every review
round of the guide checks against this list, in this order. A page passes only when every line holds.

## Clarity first

1. **Clarity is the biggest test**: against slop, against poor design, against anything that doesn't make
   sense. A newcomer reads it once and can say what this is, why it matters, what they can do now, what
   needs them and what isn't done, correctly and in their own words.
2. **Plain words.** No internal jargon without its meaning where it first appears; headings say what you
   learn, not a label; no bare ids (D-123, A4) in a lead sentence; counts only where they mean something.
3. **Human order.** What it is and why it matters → what you can do now, shown working → how to try it →
   what the agent decided alone and which to look at → what needs you → what isn't done → only then the code.

## More than the video (D-265)

4. **Never a restatement of the video.** The video is the overview; the guide is where you understand it
   properly: more concrete, more in depth, more examples, full and thorough.
5. **Worked examples** for each case: the input, what happens, the real output (from a saved run), and the
   edge cases.
6. **Diagrams** of how each part works (flows, states, what talks to what, before/after), drawn cleanly and
   readable in light and dark.
7. **The HTML used to its fullest**: things you can step through, click, compare, try; interaction only
   where it helps understanding, never decoration.
8. **Full diffs and real outputs** are there, a click away, grouped by what they do.

## The video comes first (D-264, D-246)

9. The video is always the start, in the normal player with all its interactions; the guide sits under it,
   and the video shrinks to a small player as you scroll. It is never replaced.
10. The marks in the video that lead to the guide are **subtle**: they don't take away from the video.
11. Clicking a marked thing, or "Watch this moment", moves between the two without leaving the page.

## Reviewing on the guide

12. **Highlight anything** in the guide and a text box pops up just like marking the video; the note is
    sent with the review.
13. Ask works in the guide as in the video.

## Look

14. **Visually appealing and flowing** (like the v3 prototype), calm, not chunky tables; readable on a
    phone; light and dark.
15. **Screenshots are sharp** (no blur, no upscaling); a click opens them full size.

## Honest

16. Nothing invented: where a plan's files don't say something, the page says so rather than guessing.
