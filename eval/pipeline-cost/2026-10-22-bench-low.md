# The bakery bench at low depth (2026-10-22)

The README's bakery request, once, on the `depth` branch (#35) at be0a1b2: the depth feature of #34, before the fixes
this run led to. It was measured as the [2026-10-11 bench](2026-10-11-bench.md) was:
- a fresh EC2 c7i.4xlarge, Claude Code 2.1.309;
- Opus 5.5 at medium effort for every request, checked per request in the telemetry;
- headless, with the same scripted review.

The prompt is the baseline's with one sentence added: "Make it a low-depth video." The agent wrote `depth: low` in
the brief. The bench's `--depth low` adds the sentence (`.claude/skills/pipeline-cost/bench/`).

## Results

The baseline is the fixed branch of 2026-10-11, the last bakery bench. It ran every check, as a brief with no depth
line does now (high).

| | 2026-10-11, fixed (every check) | low depth |
|---|---:|---:|
| **First video, wall** | **35.9 min** | **13.2 min** |
| agent-min: plan / build / verify (with rework) | 2.7 / 18.9 / 20.3 | 2.4 / 10.8 / — |
| builds (failed) | 7 (4) | 4 (2) |
| tokens in (with cache) / out | 16.2M / 146k | 5.7M / 47k |
| cost | $9.53 | $3.29 |
| **Revision, wall** | **13.6 min** | **7.1 min** |
| cost | $4.08 | $1.53 |
| scenes | 18 | 8, then 7 |
| **Variety** (layouts over scenes) | 10 / 18 | 7 / 8 |

The first video came 63% sooner and cost 65% less: $4.82 over both episodes, against $13.61. At low, verify runs
inside the build (about 25 s a build), and no fresh eyes follow it. The video is shorter by design: 2:20 and 8
scenes, against the baseline's 18. This measures the depth, not the same video made cheaper.

## The depth held

- 8 scenes, then 7 after the review; about 2:20 watched. The length stage said "a plan video at low depth: aim
  1–2 min, at most 8 scenes".
- No fresh eyes: the build's `fresh-eyes` stage said "none at low depth", and no `fresh-eyes` command ran.
- No strict checks: check-terms' findings were warnings.
- The review was taken in: the decision recorded (D-001), and a step 4 for the web address the review asked for.

## Where the 13.2 minutes went, and what was avoidable

The plan took 2.4 min, and the brief, storyboard and script 2.3. Narration ran in the background while one generator
script drew all 8 frames, about 3 min. The rest was builds and rework. About 3.8 min of it was avoidable:

- **The guide's check failed the first build, and the revision's** (a 65 s build, then the agent's fix). This bug is on
  main too:
  - The guide draws plan.md's "Decisions in force" under its own headings, and leaves out the section's words that
    are no line of it.
  - So `guide --check` failed a first plan's "None yet: this is the first plan.", and a section citing the plan's own
    decision (the revision).
  - The guide spec passed only because its fixture has a line that names no step: the page's warning about that
    line said the heading's words.
  - The agent got past it by deleting the section from plan.md, both times.
  - Fixed on #35: the page shows the section under its own heading, with its words (`lib/guide/model.mjs`,
    `templates/guide/guide.js`). A guide spec check fails without the fix.
- **The script came out at 2:53, 532 words, against low's 2 minutes.** The agent cut it after narrating, narrated 6 of
  8 lines again (69 s), and drew the frames again: about 1.5 min. The skill now says about 330 words before
  narrating.
- **A whole build (64 s) only to vary the transitions** (5 of 7 were push-slide). The skill now says to vary them in
  the storyboard, where `reelplanner variety` checks them before any build.
- Frame 7 was written blank by the agent's own generator: a 1 s stop, fair.

Without the first three, the first video would have come in about 9.5 min, under the 10-minute target. This is
unmeasured until a second run.

## The bench

The scripted reviewer posted the bare review, not the row the page posts, so `reel-intake` refused it ("names no
plan directory"). The agent filed it with `reel record` instead. The 2026-10-11 runs posted the same way. Fixed: the
reviewer posts the page's row (`planDir`, `project`, `review`), and `reel-intake --dry` takes it.

## Limits

- One run. Agent runs vary, and one run cannot say how much of a difference is noise.
- The baseline is 11 days older and ran every check, not medium: medium, the new default, is not measured yet.
- Quality is judged only by the variety count and a look at the contact sheet. The frames are plain: wireframes,
  the real `bakery.json`, the form, one quick check. Two were caught mid-fade on the sheet.
