Opus 5.5, auto mode, reelplanner 0.2.0 with #21 ([the run](../fresh-machine/2026-10-10-bakery-auto-mode.md)). The
plan video was built by one background fork; fresh eyes ran as two `claude -p` agents a round. Their own
transcripts were not kept (the machine was gone), so verify below is short by roughly 12–20 agent-min a video.

## Was the verifying worth it

- **Round 1 paid:** about 11 material findings, all from the newcomer: the phone number differed between scenes,
  the hours conflicted, "5 pages" against four, a recommendation half missing, an order email that left out the
  order.
- **Round 2 paid a little:** about 4, mostly round 1's phone fix not sticking, and two build timings disagreeing.
  A check that the same value matches across scenes would have caught these in seconds.
- **Round 3 found nothing material:** overflow and clipping, likely caused by round 2's edits.
- **The designer's findings were almost all cosmetic** (placement, balance), and each needed a written answer before
  the build passed.
- **The one thing the reviewer flagged was found and dismissed.** "No step gets the bakery a web address" was raised
  by the newcomer in all three rounds: answered "fixed" (in the video's wording only), then "kept" twice. The answers
  (fixed, meaning, kept) have no way to send a finding back to the plan, and the agent that built the video judges
  its own choices. (The review here was a stand-in's, but the same happened in a real one: see the misalignment runs.)
